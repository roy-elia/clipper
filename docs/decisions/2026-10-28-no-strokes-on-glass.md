# "No outlines or strokes on glass" means no stroke-only shapes on the shelf glass

## Decision
Confirmed by the owner on 2026-10-28 during the Liquid Glass pass.
- Not allowed: outline-only shapes drawn straight on the shelf glass, for example a stroked capsule as a drop target or a field border. Use a fill instead.
- Allowed and kept: the card selection ring with its keyline, and keylines on filled badges (such as the marked-card check). They outline cards and badges, not glass.

## How to apply
In glass audits, do not flag or remove the selection ring or badge keylines. Replace any stroke-only shape on the glass with a fill.
